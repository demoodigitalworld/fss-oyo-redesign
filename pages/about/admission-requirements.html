The Department: Surveying and Geoinformatics

DURATION:
The programme runs for 4 semesters.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS:
Admission Requirements: Eligible candidates should have possessed five (5) credit passes in relevant subjects including English Language, Mathematics and Physics and any other two (2) relevant subjects which may include Further Mathematics or Statistics, Technical Drawing or Fine Art, Economics or Government, Biology or Agricultural Science, Chemistry, Geography, Land Surveying, all obtained at O' Level SSCE/NECO/NABTEB at not more than two sittings.
UTME Subjects: English Language, Mathematics, Physics plus one relevant subject from Chemistry, Geography, Statistics or Further Mathematics, Biology or Agricultural Science, Economics or Government

Higher National Diploma (HND) - Full Time

The Department: Surveying and Geoinformatics

DURATION:
The programme runs for 4 semesters.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS:
Candidates must possess National Diploma (ND) in Surveying and Geoinformatics (or Land Surveying) at minimum of Lower Credit level and evidence of one year post-ND Industrial Attachment experience. In addition, candidates must have requisite entry qualification for the National Diploma (ND) programme. Candidates with ordinary Pass at ND level must have at least two (2) years post-ND experience.

Pre-Professional Diploma (PPD) - Full Time

The Department: Surveying and Geoinformatics

DURATION:
The programme runs for 2 semesters.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS:
Candidates must possess First degree (with Honours) with at least Second Class Lower Division in subjects cognate to Surveying, such as Mathematics, Physics, Geography with Mathematics, Geology and Geophysics. Candidates must possess basic entry requirement for National Diploma (ND) in Surveying and Geoinformatics which must include Credit Pass in English Language, Mathematics and Physics. In addition, candidates must be computer literate plus evidence of National Youth Service or Exemption Certificate.

Professional Diploma (PD) - Full Time

The Department: Surveying and Geoinformatics

DURATION:
The programme runs for 3 semesters.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS:
Candidates must possess any of the following qualifications:
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Surveying and Geoinformatics with a minimum of Lower Credit.
  • FSS, Oyo ATC II with equivalent level of Credit passes in both Theory and Practical.
  • In addition, evidence of National Youth Service or Exemption Certificate must be attached.
Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) - Full Time

The Department: GIS

DURATION:
The programme runs for 2 semesters.
ENTRY REQUIREMENTS:
The programme is opened to holders of First degree or HND in the fields of Sciences, Engineering, Technology, Education and Business Management such as Surveying, Geography, Physics, Computer Science, Town Planning, Estate Management, Geology, Civil Engineering, Statistics, Forestry, Cartography, Architecture, Educational Management, Business Administration and Marketing. In addition, there must be evidence of National Youth Service or Exemption Certificate and evidence of computer literacy.
