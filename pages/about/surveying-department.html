Welcome Message

The Department: Surveying and Geoinformatics

Surveying and Geoinformatics Department has put in place the following academic programmes:

  • National Diploma (ND) in Surveying and Geoinformatics. (4 Semesters)
  • Higher National Diploma (HND) in Surveying and Geoinformatics (4 Semesters)
  • Pre-Professional Diploma (PPD) in Surveying and Geoinformatics (2 Semesters)
  • Professional Diploma (PD) in Surveying and Geoinformatics (3 Semesters)

    • The programmes are to produce technicians, technologists and professionals in the field of Surveying and Geoinformatics for the national economy and this is in line with the mission statement and objectives of the school.
